About

About Us

For over 40 years, Manstal Limited has been delivering high-quality Mechanical and Electrical building services to clients across the UK, spanning commercial, retail, education, healthcare, industrial, public sector, and residential projects. Our expertise covers the full project lifecycle — from design and pre-construction, through installation and commissioning, to ongoing maintenance — giving clients a single, trusted partner from concept to completion.

Our Approach

Our approach is collaborative by design. We work closely with developers, main contractors, consultants and end users, taking the time to understand the specific demands of each project before we begin, delivering efficient, cost-effective solutions whatever the scale or complexity.

We're proud of the scale we operate at without losing the personal touch: projects ranging from £50,000 to £10 million, a team of 50+ skilled M&E staff, and circa 75–100 projects delivered nationwide every year. It's the reason more than half our clients have stayed with us for 15+ years. We hold NICEIC, CHAS, Constructionline Gold and SafeContractor accreditations.

Sustainability is central to how we build. We help clients cut environmental impact and improve building performance through low-carbon technologies such as heat pumps, EV charging infrastructure and Building Management Systems (BMS), supporting smarter, more energy-efficient buildings for the future.

At Manstal Limited, our commitment to continuous improvement and engineering excellence drives everything we do : helping us build lasting value for every client, on every project.
