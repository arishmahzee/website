About

About Us

For over 40 years, Manstal Limited has been delivering high-quality Mechanical and Electrical building services to clients across the UK, spanning commercial, retail, education, healthcare, industrial, public sector, and residential projects. Our expertise covers the full project lifecycle — from design and pre-construction, through installation and commissioning, to ongoing maintenance — giving clients a single, trusted partner from concept to completion.

Our Approach

Our approach is collaborative by design. We work in close partnership with developers, main contractors, consultants, and end users, taking the time to understand the unique demands of every project before we begin. This attention to detail, combined with genuine technical expertise, allows us to deliver efficient, cost-effective, and sustainable solutions — whatever the scale or complexity.

We hold ourselves to the highest standards of health, safety, and quality, and we take pride in delivering projects safely, on time, and within budget. It's this consistency that has earned us long-term relationships with our clients — more than 50% of whom have worked with us for over 15 years.

Sustainability sits at the core of how we work. We help clients reduce environmental impact and improve building performance through low-carbon technologies such as heat pumps, EV charging infrastructure, and Building Management Systems (BMS), supporting smarter, more energy-efficient buildings for the future.

At Manstal Limited, our commitment to continuous improvement and engineering excellence drives everything we do — helping us build lasting value for every client, on every project.
