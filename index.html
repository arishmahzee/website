About Us

Manstal Limited is a Birmingham-based Mechanical and Electrical building services contractor with over 40 years of industry experience. We deliver engineering solutions across commercial, retail, education, healthcare, industrial, and residential sectors throughout the UK. With more than half our clients working with us for 15+ years, our reputation is built on trust, reliability, and consistent quality.
